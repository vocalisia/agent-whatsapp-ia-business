## 5 Real Estate-Specific Use Cases

### 1. 24/7 Lead Qualification

Every enquiry is an opportunity. The AI agent handles all enquiries instantly, no matter the time.

**Automated qualification questions:**
- Type of property sought (flat / house / land / commercial space).
- Desired size and number of rooms.
- Maximum budget (purchase or rent).
- Preferred location.
- Timeline (urgent / 3 months / 6+ months).
- Current situation (tenant / homeowner / first-time buyer).
- Financing (cash / mortgage / pre-approval obtained?).

In 5–7 exchanges, the AI agent automatically categorises the lead:
- **Hot** (confirmed budget + short timeline + financing) → Agent alert + viewing proposed.
- **Warm** (real project but longer timeline) → Monthly nurturing.
- **Cold** (curious, no real project) → Light informational sequence.

### 2. Automated Viewing Scheduling

The AI agent integrates with your agents’ calendars in real time and proposes viewing slots without back-and-forth phone calls.

**Automated workflow:**
1. "Hot" lead detected.
2. AI agent: *"Great, I can offer you a viewing for this property on Tuesday at 10 AM or Thursday at 5:30 PM. Which works best for you?"*
3. Client chooses → Automatic confirmation + Google Calendar invite + reminder 1 day before.

**Impact:** Time spent scheduling per viewing: 18 minutes → **2 minutes**. Viewing attendance rate: **+31%** (thanks to reminders).

### 3. Property Photo Analysis

Prospects often send photos of properties they’ve seen elsewhere for your opinion. The AI agent analyses these photos and provides relevant feedback.

**Examples:**
- Photo of a façade → Estimate of construction type, era, potential.
- Photo of a view → Identification of the neighbourhood, comments on orientation.
- Photo of a renovation quote → Assessment of feasibility before purchase.

This level of engagement builds trust and accelerates conversion.

### 4. Automated Follow-Up for Cold Prospects

A prospect who viewed a property three months ago but hasn’t signed isn’t lost. The AI agent handles intelligent follow-ups.

**Reactivation sequence:**
- Day 30: *"Hi [First Name], how’s your search going? We’ve just listed a property that matches your criteria."*
- Day 60: New matching property + viewing invitation.
- Day 90: *"Interest rates have changed — would you like to revisit your mortgage simulation?"*
- Day 180: Final contact before closing the file.

**Results:** 22% of cold prospects convert within six months using this automated sequence.

### 5. Vendor Support

The AI agent doesn’t just work with buyers. It also manages relationships with your vendors.

**What the AI agent does for vendors:**
- Weekly automated updates on viewings and feedback.
- Answers questions about the sales process.
- Collects documents (surveys, title deeds) via WhatsApp.
- Sends alerts when offers are received, including a summary of the offer.
