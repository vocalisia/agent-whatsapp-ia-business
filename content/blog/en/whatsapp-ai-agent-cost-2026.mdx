## The 4 Cost Components of a WhatsApp AI Agent

Before comparing prices, you need to understand what you're actually paying for.

### 1. WhatsApp Business API Costs (Meta)

Meta charges per conversation category:
- **Service conversations** (customer-initiated): ~€0.047/conversation
- **Marketing conversations** (your outbound messages): ~€0.089/conversation
- **Authentication conversations**: ~€0.027/conversation
- **Utility conversations** (transactional): ~€0.055/conversation

An SMB with 500 conversations/month pays roughly **€25–45/month** in Meta fees. This is unavoidable with any provider using the official API.

### 2. AI Processing Costs (LLM)

Every message your AI agent processes consumes compute. The cost depends on:
- The AI model chosen (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5)
- The length of your conversations
- Your message volume

Average: **€0.002 to €0.01 per message exchange**.

For 500 conversations averaging 8 messages = 4,000 messages = **€8–40/month** in AI costs.

### 3. Platform and Infrastructure Costs

The provider charges for their platform, hosting, integrations, and maintenance. This is the most variable component.

### 4. Setup and Onboarding Costs

One-time costs for configuration, knowledge base building, and integrations. Highly variable by provider.

## Hidden Costs: What to Watch For

Many providers advertise low entry prices but hide costs elsewhere.

**Questions you MUST always ask:**

✅ Is setup/onboarding free or charged separately?
✅ Are integrations (CRM, calendar) included in the price?
✅ Are there limits on messages or conversations?
