## No-code solutions for automating WhatsApp: popular options in 2026

No-code tools promise to simplify business operations by enabling automation without writing a single line of code. But how effective are they for WhatsApp?

### Zapier: the pioneer of automated integration
Zapier is often the first solution businesses consider. It allows WhatsApp to connect with hundreds of other applications via "Zaps."

- Advantages:
  - Quick setup.
  - Wide range of connectors (CRM, calendars, etc.).
  - Intuitive interface.

- Limitations:
  - Not suitable for complex or dynamic conversations.
  - Exponential costs with added workflows.
  - Unable to integrate context-based or customer intent-driven responses.

### Make (formerly Integromat): the visual alternative
Make offers a more visual approach to automation. You "draw" your integration scenarios between WhatsApp and other tools.

- Advantages:
  - Flexibility for multi-step processes.
  - Good documentation.

- Limitations:
  - Longer learning curve.
  - Lack of native support for advanced WhatsApp Business APIs.
  - Relies entirely on static scenarios — ineffective for unexpected conversations.

### ManyChat: the illusion of a "simple" chatbot
ManyChat promises to create no-code chatbots to manage conversations on WhatsApp. It seems ideal, but...

- Advantages:
  - Ease of creating simple automated responses.
  - Marketing tools (segmentation, tags).

- Limitations:
  - No real understanding of natural language.
  - Incapable of handling complex or multilingual interactions.
  - Reliance on pre-designed templates.

If a customer asks a complex question like "Is this product available in size XL and can it be delivered before the 15th?", the system will return a generic response, frustrating the user.
