## The 6 Must-Have Automation Sequences

### 1. Abandoned Cart Recovery

70% of e-commerce carts are abandoned. This is your biggest revenue leak — and the easiest to recover.

**Optimal sequence:**
- **T+30 min:** *"Hi [First Name], you left [product] in your cart. Need help completing your order?"*
- **T+4h:** If no response: *"Your cart is still available. I’ve reserved [product] for you until midnight tonight."*
- **T+24h:** If still abandoned: *"Last chance: [product] is almost out of stock. Here’s a 10% discount code valid for 2 hours: RETURN10"*

**Fashion e-commerce client results:** Recovery rate improved from 19% → **31%**. Additional revenue: +€8,400/month.

### 2. Order Confirmation and Tracking

Replace generic confirmation emails with personalised, interactive WhatsApp messages.

**Sequence:**
- **Order confirmed:** Summary + "Modify my order" button + ETA
- **Shipped:** Tracking link + courier name + delivery window
- **On the way:** Day-before alert: *"Your parcel is arriving tomorrow between 2 PM and 4 PM."*
- **Delivered:** *"Your order has arrived! Is everything perfect?"* → If "No," escalate to customer service immediately.

**Results:** Post-delivery customer service contacts: **-45%**. Delivery satisfaction score: **4.7/5**.

### 3. Proactive Customer Service and Returns Management

Address issues before they escalate into negative reviews.

**Sequence triggered by a poor delivery rating:**
1. AI agent detects return/complaint → requests a photo of the issue.
2. AI vision analyses the photo (e.g., transport damage, wrong item, defect).
3. Agent proposes a solution: express replacement/refund/discount code.
4. Return label automatically generated, if applicable.
5. Follow-up until full resolution.

**Results:** Resolution rate without human intervention: **74%**. Average Trustpilot rating: **+0.6 stars**.

### 4. Loyalty and Repeat Purchase Programmes

The most profitable customer is the one you’ve already converted.

**Loyalty sequences:**
- **Day 7 post-purchase:** Request for Google/Trustpilot review.
- **Day 30:** Complementary product recommendation based on previous purchase.
- **Day 60:** Referral programme: *"Invite a friend and you’ll both earn €15."*
- **Day 90:** Repeat purchase prompt for consumables: *"Time to restock your [product]?"*
- **Customer birthday:** Exclusive 24-hour offer.

**Results:** 12-month customer lifetime value (LTV): **+28%**. Active referral rate: **12%** of contacted customers.

### 5. Flash Sale Stock Clearance Campaigns

Clear inventory fast with hyper-targeted campaigns boasting 98% open rates.

**Optimal mechanics:**
- Segmentation by purchase history (who bought what).
- Personalised message with product image.
- Genuine urgency (limited stock + countdown timer).
- Direct WhatsApp response: *"I’ll take it"* → Agent completes the order.

**Cosmetics e-commerce client results:** 200 units cleared in **4 hours**. Campaign ROI: **18:1**.

### 6. Reactivating Dormant Customers

A customer who hasn’t purchased in 90+ days isn’t lost — just inactive.

**Reactivation sequence:**
- Automatically identify dormant customers (90 days without purchase).
- Personalised message based on their last purchase: *"It’s been a while! Here’s what’s new in [preferred category]..."*
- Reactivation offer: exclusive "loyal customer" discount.
- If no response after 7 days: soft closure and respectful opt-out.

**Results:** Reactivation rate: **18%** (vs 3% via email). ROI: **9:1**.
