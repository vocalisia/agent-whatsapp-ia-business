## What Is a WhatsApp Business AI Agent?

Unlike a rigid menu chatbot, a WhatsApp Business AI agent uses a **Large Language Model (LLM)** to understand your customers' messages in natural language — regardless of how they're phrased, what language they're in, or what format they use (text, voice, image).

**What a WhatsApp Business AI agent can do:**
- Answer frequently asked questions 24/7 with personalised responses
- Qualify incoming leads using BANT, MEDDIC or custom frameworks
- Book appointments directly in your calendar (Google Calendar, Calendly, Doctolib)
- Process orders and provide real-time tracking
- Escalate complex conversations to a human agent with full context
- Send proactive campaigns (abandoned cart, reminders, promotions)

## How Does a WhatsApp Business AI Agent Work?

The architecture of a WhatsApp Business AI agent relies on three essential components:

**1. The WhatsApp Business API**
The foundation. Without it, no AI agent. You need a verified phone number, a Meta Business account, and either direct access to the API or a third-party provider (Twilio, MessageBird, 360dialog).

**2. The AI Processing Layer**
This is where the intelligence lives. When a customer sends a message, it's transmitted to an LLM (GPT-4, Claude, Llama) which interprets the intent, queries your knowledge base, and generates a contextualised response.

**3. The Integration Layer**
Your AI agent must be able to act — not just respond. This means integrations with your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive), your booking tools, your inventory management, your ticketing system.

## Implementation: The 5-Step Method

**Step 1 — Define your use cases (Week 1)**
Don't try to automate everything at once. Start with the highest-volume, most repetitive request (typically: hours, tracking, pricing). 80% of volume, 20% of complexity.

**Step 2 — Set up your WhatsApp Business API (Week 1-2)**
Verify your Meta Business account. Choose your provider. Configure your verified number. Estimated time: 3-7 business days.

**Step 3 — Train your knowledge base (Week 2-3)**
Document your FAQs, your processes, your products. The richer your knowledge base, the more accurate your agent. Minimum 50 Q&A pairs to start.

**Step 4 — Integrate your systems (Week 3-4)**
Connect your CRM, your calendar, your ticketing system. Test each integration individually before going live.

**Step 5 — Test, measure, optimise (Week 4+)**
Go live with a small volume (10% of traffic). Monitor the escalation rate (target: below 20%). Enrich your knowledge base weekly.

## Legal Framework: GDPR and WhatsApp

Using a WhatsApp AI agent requires compliance with:

- **Explicit prior consent** (opt-in) before sending any commercial message
- **Information** about the automated nature of responses
- **Easy unsubscribe** (opt-out) accessible at any time
- **Data retention** for a limited period, documented

Note: WhatsApp prohibits sending unsolicited commercial messages. Proactive campaigns require prior opt-in from the customer.
