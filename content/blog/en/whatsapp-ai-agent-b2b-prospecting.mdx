## How to Integrate a WhatsApp AI Agent into Your B2B Prospecting

### Smart Cadences: Engage, Don’t Spam
A good WhatsApp AI agent knows when and how to contact your prospects. Unlike traditional email, where sending three follow-ups in five days might be acceptable, WhatsApp requires carefully calibrated timing:
- **First Contact**: A short, personalised message—never generic.
- **Gentle Follow-Up**: 48 to 72 hours later, adding value (e.g., a link to a white paper or an exclusive offer).
- **Final Attempt**: After a week, without pushing. If there’s no response, stop sending messages to protect your brand image.

### Warm-Up: Lay the Groundwork Before Prospecting
Jumping straight into outbound prospecting on WhatsApp without preparation? A recipe for failure. An effective warm-up includes:
- **Clear Opt-In**: Use an email campaign or a form on your website ([see our GDPR WhatsApp guide](/en/blog/gdpr-whatsapp-ai-guide)).
- **Precise Database Segmentation**: Use CRM tags in tools like HubSpot or Salesforce to target each segment accurately.
- **Message Template Testing**: Simple A/B testing to optimise response rates.

## Legal Compliance and Opt-In: What You Need to Know

### WhatsApp B2B and Opt-In: The Golden Rule
In the UK and across Europe, any prospecting via WhatsApp requires prior consent. This is non-negotiable. Here’s what GDPR regulations specify:
- **Mandatory Opt-In**: Businesses must obtain explicit consent from prospects before sending messages.
- **Proof of Consent**: Keep records in your CRM systems.
- **Easy Unsubscribe Options**: Every message must include a clear way to opt out of future contact.

This makes capturing opt-ins from the outset crucial, using methods such as:
- A dedicated form on your website.
- An initial informational message with an explicit consent request.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can WhatsApp Completely Replace Email for B2B Prospecting?
No. WhatsApp is complementary. Email remains relevant for long-form documents or formal communications. WhatsApp excels at quick, engaging interactions.

### How Can I Prevent My WhatsApp Messages from Being Seen as Spam?
Stick to appropriate cadences, personalise your messages, and secure explicit opt-ins. Your AI agent should also adjust its frequency based on responses—or lack thereof.

### What’s the Best Way to Obtain Opt-Ins on WhatsApp?
Set up dedicated forms on your website or initiate contact via email with an explicit consent request. Opt-ins are legally required under GDPR.
