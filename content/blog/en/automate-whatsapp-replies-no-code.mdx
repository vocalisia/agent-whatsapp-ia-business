## Why Automating WhatsApp Without Coding Is Appealing but Misleading

### Immediate Benefit: Accessibility and Lower Costs  
No-code platforms promise simplicity: creating automations without writing a single line of code. Tools like Zapier, Make (formerly Integromat), ManyChat, and n8n allow SMEs and startups to quickly integrate WhatsApp Business workflows. Typical examples include:  
- **Automated welcome messages**: "Hello, thank you for reaching out!"  
- **Redirecting to web pages**: Sending product links or forms.  
- **Simple reminders**: Appointment confirmations or follow-ups.  

### The Limitations When Needs Become Complex  
These no-code tools shine for basic tasks, but as your requirements grow, their shortcomings become evident:  
- **No contextual understanding**: No-code tools fail to grasp the intent behind messages. For instance, "What are your opening hours?" might be handled, but "Are you open on Sundays?" remains misunderstood.  
- **Rigid workflows**: Scenarios are fixed. If a customer deviates from the script, the automation breaks down.  
- **Time-consuming maintenance**: Any change in your products or services requires manual adjustments to every automation.
