## Outbound prospecting on WhatsApp: rules and pitfalls

### Respecting B2B opt-in laws
Unlike B2C, B2B prospecting is a bit more flexible legally. You can contact a business without the recipient’s explicit consent, provided that:
1. The message is relevant to their professional activity.
2. You always provide a clear opt-out option.

However, it’s crucial to remain compliant with regulations like GDPR (or CNIL in France). Discover the details in our comprehensive guide: [GDPR and WhatsApp AI Business](/fr/blog/rgpd-whatsapp-ia-guide).

### Common mistakes to avoid
- **Non-personalized messages:** Sending a generic message is the quickest way to be ignored.
- **Overly aggressive cadence:** Bombarding a prospect with WhatsApp messages feels like spam. Never exceed **3 messages per week**.
- **Ignoring responses:** If a prospect takes the time to reply, your AI must be capable of interacting intelligently.

## How to build an effective outbound strategy with WhatsApp

### Step 1: Identify your audience
It all starts with precise segmentation. Use your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.) to identify:
- Companies recently engaged with your brand.
- Industries where you’ve already proven yourself.
- Contacts who’ve interacted with your email or LinkedIn campaigns.

### Step 2: Create a message sequence
To avoid spamming, your AI agent must follow a well-defined cadence. Example:
1. **Day 1:** Quick introduction of your offer, with an open-ended question to engage.
2. **Day 3:** Share a client case study or relevant statistic.
3. **Day 7:** Offer a call or free demo.
