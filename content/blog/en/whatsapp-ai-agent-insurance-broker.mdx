## Automating Lead Qualification with AI

Lead qualification is often a lengthy and error-prone process. With an AI agent, brokers can automate this step right from the first interaction.

### Targeted Questions from the First Exchange
A WhatsApp AI agent can ask predefined questions to filter prospects:
- What type of insurance are you looking for? (car, home, professional health, etc.)
- Are you currently covered by another policy?
- What’s your budget?

These questions help segment leads and prioritise high-potential prospects. [Discover our tips for optimising your WhatsApp B2B campaigns.](/en/blog/whatsapp-b2b-lead-qualification)

### Customised Scenarios Tailored to Your Business
Every broker has unique needs. For example:
- **Car insurance**: The agent can directly request vehicle details (make, model, registration number).
- **Home insurance**: Collects information on property size, location, and ownership status.
- **Health/professional insurance**: Follows up on specific needs related to health coverage or liability insurance.

## Automated Quote Follow-Ups to Boost Conversions

Sending a quote isn’t enough—clients may forget or hesitate. An AI agent can automate follow-ups while personalising messages to suit your context.

### Optimised Timing
- Follow-up 1 (Day +2): "Hello, have you had a chance to review our quote? I’m available for any questions."
- Follow-up 2 (Day +5): "We currently have limited-time offers. Your quote is expiring soon. Need assistance?"
- Final follow-up (Day +14): "Last reminder: the quote is valid until [DATE]. Let me know if I can help."
