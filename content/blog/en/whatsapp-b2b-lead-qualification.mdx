## The Automated BANT Method on WhatsApp

### B — Budget

**Questions to ask:**
- *"To recommend the most suitable solution, could you share the budget range you’ve allocated for this project?"*
- *"Have you already budgeted for this initiative this quarter?"*

**Qualification logic:**
- Defined and approved budget → Score +2  
- Approximate budget → Score +1  
- No budget → Score 0 → Long-term nurturing  

### A — Authority

**Questions to ask:**
- *"Who else will be involved in the final decision?"*
- *"Are you the decision-maker, or are there other stakeholders involved?"*

**Qualification logic:**
- Final decision-maker → Score +2  
- Influencer with access to the decision-maker → Score +1  
- Curious party with no influence → Score 0 → Educational content  

### N — Need

**Questions to ask:**
- *"What specific problem are you trying to solve?"*
- *"What prompted you to look for a solution now?"*
- *"What have you already tried, and why didn’t it work?"*

**Qualification logic:**
- Clearly expressed pain point + urgency → Score +2  
- Vague but real need → Score +1  
- Exploring without a defined problem → Score 0  

### T — Timeline

**Questions to ask:**
- *"When would you like to have this solution in place?"*
- *"Is there a deadline or event driving this timeline?"*

**Qualification logic:**
- Decision within 30 days → Score +2  
- Decision within 90 days → Score +1  
- No defined urgency → Score 0  

### Final Score and Automated Routing

| Total Score | Classification | Automatic Action |
|-------------|----------------|------------------|
| 7–8         | Hot lead 🔥    | Immediate alert to salesperson + meeting proposed |
| 4–6         | Warm lead ☀️   | 30-day nurturing sequence + salesperson notified |
| 1–3         | Cold lead ❄️   | Educational content + follow-up in 90 days |
| 0           | Disqualified   | Clean archive |
